Research areas

The fields that shape my research, design work and teaching.

Artificial intelligence and motion data

Applying artificial intelligence to motion data and designing intelligent measurement and analysis systems.

Biomedical engineering and rehabilitation

Developing systems that assess patient movement during physiotherapy, using gamification to raise patient engagement, and measuring foot pressure while walking.

Biological and medical technologies

Research on a bio-adhesive based on freshwater algae for bone and cartilage repair, and on chitosan and PNIPAM nanoparticles for targeted glioblastoma treatment.

Metrology and instrumentation

Combining electromagnetic and ultrasonic technologies for non-destructive evaluation, authentication and purity assessment of precious metals.
