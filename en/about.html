About

Mehdi Lashgari

Industrial engineer, researcher in emerging technologies, director of Unlimited Science Learners, and active in education and innovation development.

Professional profile

Mehdi Lashgari is an industrial engineer, a researcher in emerging technologies, the director of Unlimited Science Learners, and active in education and innovation development.

His professional work focuses on connecting engineering, emerging technologies, artificial intelligence and the life sciences. Alongside his managerial and teaching roles, he works on identifying, nurturing and guiding young innovative talent and supporting student inventors.

His interests and areas of activity include artificial intelligence, medical technologies, rehabilitation systems, biomedical engineering, assistive equipment and the development of low-cost technological solutions.

Education

  • BSc in Industrial Engineering — Manufacturing and Production, Islamic Azad University, South Tehran Branch, 2001–2006.
  • PhD in Electronics — Telecommunications, doctoral student, [university name].

Management and teaching

He is the chief executive and director of Unlimited Science Learners, where he oversees educational and research programmes, guides student innovation projects, identifies and supports young talent, mentors inventor teams for international exhibitions and competitions, builds relations with organisations, federations and international scientific bodies, and develops scientific and technological cooperation.

Alongside this role he works as a teacher, researcher and scientific mentor in secondary schools in Alborz province: teaching and guiding students in science and technology, accompanying student teams as they develop an idea into a presentable project, and advising on scientific documentation and invention disclosure.

Research activities

Biomedical engineering and rehabilitation

  • Development of intelligent systems for assessing human movement with artificial intelligence
  • Design of systems that assess patient movement during physiotherapy
  • Use of gamification to increase patient participation in rehabilitation exercises
  • Development of methods for measuring foot pressure while walking
  • Investigation of low-cost machine vision systems for motor performance assessment

Artificial intelligence and electronic engineering

  • Use of artificial intelligence algorithms for processing motion data
  • Design of intelligent measurement and analysis systems
  • Combining electromagnetic and ultrasonic technologies for examining precious metals

Biological and medical technologies

  • Research on a bio-adhesive based on freshwater algae for bone and cartilage repair
  • Investigation of chitosan and PNIPAM nanoparticles for targeted glioblastoma treatment
  • Study of technologies for assessing and correcting patients’ motor performance
